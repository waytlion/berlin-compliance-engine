# Berlin Short-Term Rental Regulations
## Zweckentfremdungsverbot-Gesetz (ZwVbG) Summary

### Key Requirements

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| **Wohnraumschutznummer** | Mandatory registration number from district office (Bezirksamt) |
| **Primary Residence Rule** | Max **60 days/year** tourist rental without permit |
| **Room Rental** | Can rent single room if owner occupies ≥50% of space |
| **Permit Required** | Full apartment rentals > 60 days need district approval |
| **Fines** | Up to **€500,000** for violations |

---

## Legal Scenarios

### ✅ ALLOWED (No Permit Needed)
1. Renting your **primary residence** to tourists for **≤60 days/year** (with Wohnraumschutznummer)
2. Renting a **single room** while occupying ≥50% of apartment (incl. kitchen/bath)
3. **Temporary furnished housing** for workers/students relocating to Berlin (min 2 months)

### ⚠️ WARNING (Requires Permit)
1. Tourist rentals at primary residence **>60 days/year**
2. Full apartment subletting for medium-term (1-6 months) without tenant establishing residency
3. Any short-term rental without Wohnraumschutznummer

### ❌ DENIED (Illegal)
1. Renting **entire apartment** as holiday home without permit
2. No Wohnraumschutznummer displayed in listing
3. Commercial vacation rental operation
4. Secondary residence used for short-term rentals
5. Rentals without reporting to Bezirksamt

---

## Required Documents for Compliance
1. **Wohnraumschutznummer** - Registration with district office
2. **Permit (Genehmigung)** - If exceeding 60-day limit
3. **Proof of Primary Residence** - Anmeldung showing property as Hauptwohnsitz
4. **Rental Duration Records** - Log of days rented per year
