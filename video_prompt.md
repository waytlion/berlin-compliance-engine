# Character Video Generation Prompt

## Context
We need a short video for a hackathon pitch. The character is "Steve", a legal expert from the company "Arbio". He should look professional, trustworthy, but modern (legal-tech).

## Scene Description
**Character**: "Steve" - Male, 30s-40s. Sharp tailored suit (navy or charcoal), no tie (modern professional). Well-groomed, confident but friendly smile.
**Setting**: High-end broadcast studio or modern office with depth of field (blurred background). Soft, warm, cinematic lighting.
**Action**: He sits at a desk or stands comfortably. He speaks directly to the camera. At the end, he gives a subtle, charming **wink**.

## Script
> "Hey, I'm Steve from Arbio. I make sure your property fulfills all the short-term rental regulations for the City of Berlin. This just takes a few seconds." -> [WINK]

## Visual Prompt for Video AI (e.g., Haiper, Runway, Sora)
> "Hyper-realistic video of a charismatic male lawyer in a modern studio. He is speaking naturally to the camera. He wears a navy suit. The lighting is cinematic. At the end of the clip, he smiles broadly and gives a confident wink to the camera. 4k resolution, shallow depth of field."

## Audio/Lip-Sync Prompt (e.g., HeyGen, adjustable settings)
> **Tone**: Professional, Reassuring, Enthusiastic but calm.
> **Speed**: Natural conversational pace (appx. 1.0x - 1.1x).
> **Emphasis**: Emphasize "all" and "just a few seconds".
